# Facility Partner Onboarding & Commercial Settlement Agreement
**Governing Agent:** `apex-legal` (Legal Compliance Expert)  
**Target:** Venue Owners in Thane, Mumbai, and Navi Mumbai  

---

## 1. Commercial Settlement & Payout Cadence
1. **Platform Commission:** Project Apex retains a nominal technological convenience fee per confirmed slot reservation.
2. **Payout Cycle:** Net venue earnings are settled within 24 hours (T+1) directly to the Venue Partner's verified bank account via IMPS/NEFT.
3. **Cancellation Compensation:** Venue Partners receive 100% of the forfeited fee for Tier 3 cancellations (<6 hours) and 50% for Tier 2 cancellations (6-24 hours).

---

## 2. Facility Operational Standards
Venue Partners warrant and agree to:
1. Maintain turf surfaces, badminton court mats, and squash glass panels in playable and clean condition.
2. Provide functional, high-lumen floodlights for all evening and night-owl matches.
3. Keep clean drinking water and sanitary changing facilities accessible to players.
4. Honor all reservations committed through Project Apex without manual double-booking.
